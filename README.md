# PetHarmony 宠程

Interactive WeChat Mini Program prototype for PetHarmony — a smart pet-care
platform combining wearable hardware (smart collar, smart bowl), gamified
training with a points system, AI food scanning, online vet consults,
nearby services map, a community feed, and a rewards mall.

## Demo

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in any modern browser. The prototype is a
single fully self-contained file: no build step, no internet connection
needed — all illustrations and icons are inline SVG.

## Features

- **Home** — pet hero card, points & daily check-in, quick-access grid, promo banners, AI mood card, wellness score, daily tasks
- **Training** — session check-ins, diet tab, expert academy video courses, weekly ranking
- **AI Food Scan** — Bitepal-style scanner with nutrition scores, macros, AI advice, and a diet log
- **Smart hardware** — collar S2 (GPS, heart rate, temperature, bark/mood AI, aroma diffuser) and bowl B1 detail pages
- **Vet** — searchable/filterable vet list, appointment card, on-demand video consult
- **Shop** — points redemption coupons, product catalog with detail pages, cart & checkout, membership plans
- **Community** — following/trending/suggested feeds, posting, likes, chat & notifications
- **Nearby map** — category-filtered pins and place cards
- **Multilingual** — one-tap switching between 中文 / English / Italiano via a draggable floating button
